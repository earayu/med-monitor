## 3. 重点产品 (有明确疗效/生存数字)

### Pasritamig/JNJ-78278343 (KLK2×CD3, Janssen)

- 来源: J Clin Oncol 2025 Aug, PMID 40450573; 阶段: phase I; 样本量: 174 participants (RP2D 安全性人群 n=45, RP2D 疗效人群 n=33)
- 疗效 (摘要原文): "In the RP2D efficacy population (n = 33), median radiographic progression-free survival was 7.85 (95% CI, 2.89 to not estimable) months, and 14 of 33 (42.4%) participants achieved a ≥50% decrease from baseline in prostate-specific antigen."
- 安全性 (摘要原文): "In the RP2D safety population (n = 45), infusion-related reactions (11/45, 24.4%), fatigue (7/45, 15.6%), cytokine release syndrome (CRS; 4/45, 8.9%, all grade 1), and lipase increase (4/45, 8.9%) were the most frequent TRAEs; all were grade 1 or 2."
- 语境/备注: mCRPC I 期 FIH 剂量递增研究; KLK2 first-in-class 靶点; v2 新增 (原 JNJ-78278343); 另一篇 PMID 41993992 为 ASCO/ESMO 2025 系统综述, 仅提及名字

### Obrixtamig/BI 764532 (DLL3×CD3, Boehringer Ingelheim)

- 来源: J Clin Oncol 2025 Sep, PMID 40706016; 阶段: phase I; 样本量: 168 patients
- 疗效 (摘要原文): "the overall response rate (ORR) was 23% (95% CI, 17.4% to 30.2%), the median duration of response (DoR) was 8.5 months (95% CI, 6.2 to not reached); Regimens B2/B3 achieved an ORR of 28% (95% CI, 20.7% to 35.9%)"
- 安全性 (摘要原文): "The most common treatment-related adverse event was cytokine release syndrome (any grade: 57%; grade ≥3: 3%); most cases occurred early and were reversible."
- 语境/备注: 非随机 I 期剂量递增/扩展研究 (DLL3+ SCLC/epNEC/LCNEC-L), JCO 发表; MTD 未达到; 另一篇 PMID 42054725 为综述, 无原始临床数据

### IBI389 (CLDN18.2×CD3, 信达生物)

- 来源: BMC Med 2026-01, PMID 41540424; 阶段: phase 1; 样本量: 121 patients
- 疗效 (摘要原文): "the ORR was 22.2% (6/27; 95% CI, 8.6-42.3) and DCR was 74.1% (20/27; 95% CI, 53.7-88.9). Median progression-free survival (PFS) was 4.3 months (95% CI, 2.7-7.2), and median overall survival (OS) was 10.3 months (95% CI, 6.6-not calculable)."
- 安全性 (摘要原文): "Cytokine release syndrome (CRS) related adverse events occurred in 72 patients (59.5%), all grades 1-2 except one grade 3 event. No neurotoxicity or treatment-related deaths were observed."
- 语境/备注: 剂量递增+扩展; n=121 为安全性全集; ORR 22.2% (6/27) 仅限 CLDN18.2 2+/3+ ≥10% 的 GC/GEJC 疗效亚组; NCT05164458; 另一篇 PMID 41969434 为临床前研究未收录

### Cibisatamab/RO6958688/RO6958689 (CEA×CD3, Roche)

- 来源: Nat Med 2026 Jul, PMID 42010119; 阶段: phase 1b; 样本量: 52 treated patients
- 疗效 (摘要原文): "Confirmed partial responses were observed in 7 out of 52 patients (13.5%)."
- 安全性 (摘要原文): "Cytokine release syndrome (CRS) occurred in 30 out of 52 patients (57.7%; grade ≥3: 2 out of 52, 3.8%); Colitis occurred in 7 out of 52 patients (13.5%), including immune-mediated enterocolitis and one fatal cytomegalovirus colitis."
- 语境/备注: cibisatamab + FAP-4-1BBL 联合用药 + obinutuzumab 预处理治疗 MSS 转移性结直肠癌的 1b 期剂量递增研究 (NCT04826003); 确认 PR 7/52 (13.5%) 来自联合方案, 非单药; 摘要未直接使用 ORR 一词; 另一篇 PMID 40108739 为 popPK/ADA 建模研究, 无疗效数据

### Acapatamab/AMG 160 (PSMA×CD3, Amgen)

- 来源: Clin Cancer Res 2024 Apr, PMID 38300720; 阶段: phase I; 样本量: 133 patients (dose exploration, n = 77; dose expansion, n = 56)
- 疗效 (摘要原文): "confirmed prostate-specific antigen (PSA) responses (PSA50) were seen in 30.4% of patients and radiographic partial responses in 7.4% (Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors 1.1). Median PSA progression-free survival (PFS) was 3.3 months [95% confidence interval (CI): 3.0-4.9], radiographic PFS per Prostate Cancer Clinical Trials Working Group 3 was 3.7 months (95% CI: 2.0-5.4)"
- 安全性 (摘要原文): "Cytokine release syndrome (CRS) was the most common treatment-emergent adverse event seen in 97.4% and 98.2% of patients in dose exploration and dose expansion, respectively; grade ≥ 3 was seen in 23.4% and 16.1%, respectively"
- 语境/备注: mCRPC 首次人体研究原始论文; PSA50 30.4% 与影像 PR 7.4% 来自剂量扩展队列 n=56 (总 n=133 不作疗效分母); 另一篇 PMID 41236620 为 World J Urol 2025 Nov 综述, 仅转引本研究数据, 未单列

### Xaluritamig/AMG 509 (STEAP1×CD3, Amgen)

- 来源: Mol Biol Rep 2026-07, PMID 42455319; 阶段: 摘要未注明; 样本量: 不适用(系统综述)
- 疗效 (摘要原文): "Xaluritamig achieved a 59% PSA50 response"
- 安全性: 摘要未报告
- 语境/备注: CRPC 免疫治疗系统综述;仅转述 Xaluritamig PSA50 数据,非原始临床试验报告; 复核注: 两个数字均来自二手综述且终点不一致 (PSA50 vs responses), 待原始临床报告
- 另见 Cancers (Basel) 2026-03 (PMID 41976311): "In metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer, xaluritamig produced ~41% responses in heavily pretreated patients."

### M701 (EpCAM×CD3, 武汉友芝友)

- 来源: Exp Hematol Oncol 2025 Nov, PMID 41275209; 阶段: 对照 II 期; 样本量: 84 patients were enrolled, with 43 in the M701 group receiving paracentesis and IP M701 infusion and 41 in the control group receiving paracentesis alone
- 疗效 (摘要原文): "median puncture-free survival (PuFS), was 75 days in the M701 group and 25 days in the control group, with a significant difference (p = 0.0065); the overall survival (OS) of patients in the M701 group was certain extended (mOS 110 days vs. 76 days, p = 0.1443, HR = 0.68). The 6-month survival rates were 33.3% and 12.1% in the two groups, respectively"
- 安全性 (摘要原文): "No additional serious adverse events (SAEs) were detected in the M701 group. The most frequent treatment-related adverse events were anemia and low white blood cell count, which were manageable"
- 语境/备注: 晚期上皮性肿瘤恶性腹水腹腔灌注研究; PuFS 75 vs 25 天差异有统计学意义 (p=0.0065), OS 110 vs 76 天 p=0.1443 未达统计学显著; 摘要未报告 ORR (终点为 PuFS/OS)

### Tarlatamab (DLL3×CD3, Amgen (获批))

- 来源: Clin Lung Cancer 2026 Jul, PMID 42501611; 阶段: 摘要未注明（回顾性真实世界研究）; 样本量: 204 patients
- 疗效 (摘要原文): "mPFS was 3.8 months(m), mTTD was 2.3 m, and mOS was 11.2 m. ECOG PS ≥ 2 compared to ECOG 0-1 was associated with shorter mPFS (2.1 vs. 4.5; P = .004); mTTD (1.6 vs. 2.8; P = .001) and mOS (3.2 vs. 13.4; P < .001)."
- 安全性: 摘要未报告
- 语境/备注: 回顾性真实世界研究 (Flatiron 数据库, 2L+ ES-SCLC); 摘要未报告 ORR; 另一篇 PMID 42449715 为综述, 仅提及 tarlatamab 获批, 无临床数据

## 4. 终止 / 撤回项目

- **TAK-186** (EGFR×CD3, Takeda): ct.gov 2 项试验终止/撤回; 文献 (MAbs 2025-12, PMID 41207862) 明确其为 "a multidomain T cell engager that has been discontinued from clinical development"。
- **Ertumaxomab** (HER2×CD3, Neovii): ct.gov 4 项试验全部终止 (最近更新 2016-05); 2023+ 仅见于 TCE 毒性系统综述 (PMID 40644745)。
- **PF-06671008** (P-cadherin×CD3, Pfizer): 唯一 I 期 NCT02659631 已终止 (2020-05); 2023+ PubMed 未检出同行评议临床结果。

## 5. 监管状态备注
